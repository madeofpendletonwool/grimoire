# Attributions

Grimoire's interface is built from other people's art. This file records who
made what, under which terms, and what Grimoire did to it.

The upstream packs themselves are **not** in this repository. `icon-packs/` is
gitignored; what ships in `web/static/assets/` is the derived output of
`scripts/build-assets.py`, which copies the one icon sheet we use and
recolours the frames into Grimoire's own palette. Anyone rebuilding the assets
needs to obtain the packs from the links below first.

---

## Pixel art

### Complete UI Book Styles Pack — TravelBook Lite
**Crusenho Agus Hennihuno** · <https://crusenho.itch.io/complete-ui-book-styles-pack>

Every framed surface in the app: the parchment panel behind answers, rules and
cards; the leather cover and page that make the sign-in screen a closed book;
the reference drawer's page with its spine and page-stack; buttons, chips,
inset fields, selection brackets and the progress bar.

*Credit is required by this licence, and the licence forbids republishing the
pack.* Grimoire ships adaptations, not the pack: `panel-stone.png`,
`field.png`, `button.png` and the rest are recoloured into Grimoire's stone and
gold, and the book cover is scaled 2× so its corner filigree survives at
interface size. The recolour maps live in `scripts/build-assets.py`.

### Shikashi's Fantasy Icons Pack v2
**Shikashi** · <https://cheekyinkling.itch.io/shikashis-fantasy-icons-pack>

The 32px sprite sheet — the spellbook in the wordmark, the scroll on New Chat,
the mana orb and crossed swords that stand for the two rule sets, the wizard's
staff beside every answer, the hand-and-scrolls that animate while the sage is
thinking, and the candle, key, hourglass and lantern in the chrome.

Free for commercial use and remixing. Many of its icons are themselves based on
designs from game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0). Shipped as the pack's own transparent
sheet, unmodified; `web/static/js/icons.js` names the cells Grimoire uses.

### Parallax Backgrounds — Caves, Dead Forest, Snowy Mountains, Plains
**Admurin** · <https://admurin.itch.io>

The chamber behind the app, selectable in Settings. Layers are used as-is and
composited at runtime: `web/static/pixel.css` pushes them through a sepia and
contrast filter so the cool violet-grey art takes Grimoire's warm colour, and a
veil above them holds a floor under text contrast.

Free for commercial use, credit appreciated but not required. The licence
forbids redistribution as a game asset and use for AI training.

## Fonts

All self-hosted in `web/static/fonts/` — Grimoire is a single binary that has
to render correctly with no route to the internet, so nothing loads from a CDN.
Fetched by `scripts/fetch-fonts.py`.

| Font | By | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [EB Garamond](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/EB+Garamond) | Georg Duffner, Octavio Pardo | OFL 1.1 | Rules prose and answers |
| [Cinzel](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel) | Natanael Gama | OFL 1.1 | Wordmark and headings |
| [Departure Mono](https://departuremono.com/) | Helena Zhang | OFL 1.1 | Keycaps, labels, badges |
| [Mana](https://mana.andrewgioia.com/) | Andrew Gioia | Font OFL 1.1, CSS MIT | Magic's mana, tap and card-type symbols |
| [Keyrune](https://keyrune.andrewgioia.com/) | Andrew Gioia | Font OFL 1.1, glyphs GPL 3.0 | Magic set symbols |

`web/static/mana.css` and `web/static/keyrune.css` are the upstream class
sheets with their `@font-face` blocks stripped; the families are declared in
`web/static/fonts.css` against the self-hosted woff2 files.
